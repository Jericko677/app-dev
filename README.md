## My Favorite Movie: Stand by Me Doraemon

**Stand by Me Doraemon** is a *3D animated fantasy-adventure film* released in 2014. It is based on the Japanese manga and anime series *Doraemon*.

### Quick Facts

- **Genre:** Animation, Fantasy, Adventure
- **Release year:** 2014
- **Country:** Japan
- **Sequel:** Stand by Me Doraemon 2 (2020)

### Story Summary

Nobita is a clumsy and lazy schoolboy who struggles in class and is always bullied. One day, Doraemon, a robot cat from the future, appears in his room. Nobita's great-great-grandson sent Doraemon back in time to help Nobita change his future so his family won't suffer from bad luck and money problems.

### Main Characters

1. Nobita
2. Doraemon
3. Shizuka
4. Gian
5. Suneo

### Why I Like It

> Doraemon is my childhood anime, and this movie brings me right back to those days.

*Doraemon* was a big part of my childhood, so watching **Stand by Me Doraemon** feels like reliving my favorite memories as a kid. The story of Nobita and Doraemon's friendship is so heartfelt that **I cry every single time I watch it**, no matter how many times I've seen it.

It reminds me of:

- the simple joy of being a kid
- the value of friendship
- never giving up, even when things are hard
